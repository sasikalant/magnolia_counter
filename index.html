Method and limitations

Each uploaded image is letterboxed to 640 × 640, normalized to RGB float values, and passed as an NCHW tensor [1, 3, 640, 640] to the selected ONNX model. Bounding boxes are filtered by confidence and non-maximum suppression (IoU 0.45).

Designed for standard, non-end-to-end Ultralytics YOLO detection exports with raw boxes and class scores. Models exported with embedded NMS, segmentation heads, custom output layouts, or a different fixed input size may require code changes. Count accuracy depends on the trained model. Dense plants in very large aerial images may require tiled inference for better results. Coverage is the union of predicted rectangles, not true canopy coverage.

Your files stay on your device during inference; model files are fetched from the site host. No model evaluation claims are made by this demo.
